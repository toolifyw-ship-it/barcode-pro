Supported Barcode & 2D Symbologies

Code 128 (High-Density Shipping & Logistics)

Code 128 is an exceptionally high-density linear barcode capable of encoding all 128 ASCII characters (numbers, letters, symbols, and control codes). It automatically switches between Subsets A, B, and C to pack numeric data densely. Used worldwide for container shipping labels, GS1-128 logistics tracking, and warehouse shelf storage.

EAN-13 & EAN-8 (Global Retail Point-of-Sale)

EAN-13 (European Article Number) is the international 13-digit standard for retail products scanned at checkout worldwide. It features a GS1 country prefix (e.g. 890 for India, 000-139 for USA/Canada, 500 for UK), manufacturer code, item number, and Modulo-10 checksum.

UPC-A & UPC-E (North American Retail Standard)

Universal Product Codes (UPC-A) consist of 12 digits used extensively across retail stores in North America. UPC-E is a zero-suppression variant designed specifically for small product containers like cosmetics and chewing gum packs.

QR Code with Custom Logo & Branding

Quick Response (QR) codes are 2D matrix symbologies storing up to 4,296 characters. Barcoder Pro enables center logo embedding, customizable error correction levels (L, M, Q, H), frame styles, and 'SCAN ME' badges for marketing materials.
